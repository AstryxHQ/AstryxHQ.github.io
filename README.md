# 💫 Astryx
## The minds of others

**Astryx** is an open access learning platform intented to shorten the gap between a college eductaion and a everyday learner. We believe that quality resources should be allowed to everyone regardless of their financial status, previous education, and social reputation.

---

## 🔆The Mission
The normal experience for people with online learning is often cluttered with extensive monetization, lack of flexibility, and less resources availible to the average user. Astryx aims to break down these barriers by giving you:

* 🗺️ **Roadmaps** – Step-by-step paths to master complex subjects from your beginning.
* 📚 **Understandable Courses** – Deep dives into college-level material without the need to spend years and money to get them.
* ✍️ **Accesible Notes** – High-quality, extensive summaries to provide a faster, lower learning curve, experience.
* 🧪 **Interactive Testing** – Self-assessment tools to track your progress at your own pace.

---

## 🛠️ The Problems We’re Solving
We are building Astryx to address the core frustrations of modern digital learners:

| **The Problem** | **The Astryx Solution** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rigid Structure** | Flexible, non-linear learning paths that adapt to your personal pace. |
| **Resource Scavenging** | A "single source of truth" combining notes, tests, and lectures in one place. |
| **Aggressive Monetization** | A student-first philosophy. While we use monetization for sustainability, education remains our primary objective, not profit. |
